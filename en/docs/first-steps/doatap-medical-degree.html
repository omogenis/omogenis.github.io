Medical and Dental Degree Recognition in Greece (DOATAP): Preparation Guide

For medical doctors and dentists holding degrees from non-EU countries, graduates of foreign medical universities (Russia, Ukraine, Belarus, Kazakhstan, Armenia, Georgia, Romania, etc.), including expatriates and Greek citizens wishing to practice in Greece, passing the written DOATAP examinations is a mandatory step for degree recognition.

Exam Key Features

The written DOATAP examinations are conducted exclusively in the Greek language in a multiple-choice question (MCQ) format. The exam enforces a negative marking system (-0.20 points per incorrect answer). Mastering Greek medical terminology is a critical factor for success.

How Do We Verify Questions?​

On DOATAP exams, exact phrasing and answer key accuracy are of critical importance. A typo or ambiguous option can cost precious points due to the negative marking penalty.

The Iatre! platform uses a two-level verification system for every question in the database (over 7,000 Medicine questions and 5,000 Dentistry questions).

Two-Level Question Verification System

  1. Official DOATAP Review Reports

After each examination session, DOATAP emails candidates official review reports containing from 150 to 200 questions from the active question bank. The medical editorial team collects these reports, cross-references the items, and assigns them the Official Verification status.

  1. Community Feedback & Flagging

Platform users can flag any question when inaccuracies are detected. Every report is reviewed by physicians against medical literature with real-time updates.

Acetaminophen (paracetamol) hepatotoxicity is caused by:

A. Αλληλεπιδράσεις με ΜΣΑΦ(Interactions with NSAIDs)
B. Ανεπαρκή μεταβολισμό από ένζυμα CYP (φάσης 1)(Inadequate Phase 1 CYP metabolism)
C. Ανεπαρκή μεταβολισμό από ένζυμα UGT (φάσης 2)(Inadequate Phase 2 UGT metabolism)
D. Παραγωγή τοξικών μεταβολιτών από ένζυμα CYP (φάσης 1) ✓(Production of toxic Phase 1 CYP metabolites)
E. Παραγωγή τοξικών μεταβολιτών από ένζυμα UGT (φάσης 2)(Production of Phase 2 UGT metabolites)

💡 Clinical Rationale:

Hepatotoxicity is caused by the reactive toxic metabolite N-acetyl-p-benzoquinone imine (NAPQI) involving CYP2E1 (phase 1), which depletes glutathione stores when therapeutic doses are exceeded.

Step-by-Step Preparation Plan for Doctors​

Action Plan
  1. Application Submission: Register your application on the portal e-doatap.gr.
  2. Start Preparation: Begin with fundamental subjects (anatomy, physiology, pharmacology) to master Greek medical terms.
  3. Parallel Translation: Enable the translation feature on Iatre! to map familiar medical concepts to Greek terminology.
  4. Timer Practice: Complete practice tests with the negative marking simulator enabled 2–3 weeks before the exam.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)​

Where do questions in the Iatre! database come from and how are they verified?

Questions come from official result reports (containing 150–200 questions) that DOATAP emails to candidates after each exam. The editorial team enters them into the database, adds explanations, and updates them upon receiving feedback from users.

How does parallel translation work?

In practice mode, you can turn on online translation. The question text, answer options, and explanation are displayed side-by-side in your selected language (e.g., Russian or English), helping you quickly memorize Greek terms.

Is there free access?

Yes, registering on iatre.gr grants access to demo tests for all sections.
