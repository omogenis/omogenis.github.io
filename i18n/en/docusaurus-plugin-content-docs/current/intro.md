## [First Steps in Greece](/docs/category/первые-шаги-в-греции)

Essential guides for initial settlement, legal status, tax registration, and academic degree recognition upon arrival in Greece:

* **[First Steps in Greece: Checklist & Roadmap](/docs/first-steps)**  
  Step-by-step checklist of initial administrative procedures after arriving in Greece: ID/passport, Greek Tax Identification Number (AFM), social security (AMKA), bank account opening, and degree recognition.
* **[Issuing AFM & Taxisnet Credentials Online](/docs/first-steps/afm-online)**  
  Step-by-step guide for issuing a Greek Tax Number (AFM) and Taxisnet credentials (Kleidarithmos) online via myAADE and gov.gr without an in-person visit.
* **[Medical & Dental Degree Recognition (DOATAP)](/docs/first-steps/doatap-medical-degree)**  
  Comprehensive guide for international doctors and dentists: recognition procedure via e-DOATAP, qualification exams, and medical association registration.

## Official Portals & Useful Links

:::info Official Greek Government Services
* **[Citizenship Application Status (YPES)](https://pf.emigrants.ypes.gr/pfiua/)** — Official status tracker at the Ministry of Interior.
* **[Greek Independent Authority for Public Revenue (AADE)](https://www.aade.gr/)** — Tax portal, Taxisnet, and myAADElive.
* **[National Academic Recognition Center (DOATAP)](https://www.doatap.gr/)** — Online degree recognition submission portal.
* **[Center for the Greek Language (KEG)](https://www.greek-language.gr/certification/)** — Greek language proficiency certification exams.
:::
