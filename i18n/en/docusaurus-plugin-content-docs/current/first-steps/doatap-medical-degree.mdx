# DOATAP Medical & Dental Degree Recognition in Greece: Preparation Guide

For medical and dental practitioners who completed their degree at non-EU or foreign universities (United Kingdom, Romania, Bulgaria, Italy, Russia, Armenia, Georgia, etc.), including expatriates and Greek citizens, degree recognition via the written **DOATAP exams** is the mandatory step to practice in Greece.

:::note Key Exam Requirements
The official DOATAP written examinations are administered exclusively in the **Greek language** using a multiple-choice format. Exams enforce a **negative marking penalty of -0.20 points per incorrect answer**. Mastering Greek medical terminology is essential for success.
:::

---

## Step-by-Step Preparation Roadmap

:::info Action Steps
1. **Application Submission**: Submit your degree documents via the official portal [e-doatap.gr](https://www.doatap.gr).
2. **Preclinical Core Focus**: Begin with Anatomy, Physiology, and Pharmacology to build foundational Greek medical vocabulary.
3. **Side-by-Side Dual Language Practice**: Enable the translation helper on [Iatre!](https://iatre.gr) to connect English terminology directly with Greek exam phrasing.
4. **Timed Mock Simulations**: Perform practice tests with active negative marking 2-3 weeks prior to the exam date to establish your risk strategy.
:::
