Issuing an AFM and Taxisnet credentials online

The Tax Identification Number (AFM — Αριθμός Φορολογικού Μητρώου) is a unique 9-digit tax identifier in Greece (example: 123456789). Delivered alongside the AFM are your Taxisnet credentials (Kleidarithmos / Κλειδάριθμος) — the passcode required to activate your online account on the AADE tax portal.

Through the myAADE digital platform, natural persons can apply for an AFM and receive their Taxisnet credentials without visiting a local tax office (DOY) in person.

Required Documents & Data​

  1. Identity Document:
    • Valid International Passport (for foreign residents and omogeneis prior to receiving identity cards)
    • Greek National ID card / EDTO card
  2. Contact Details:
    • Active personal E-mail address
    • Mobile phone number
  3. Tax Representative (if applicable):
    • Applicants residing permanently outside Greece must provide details of a local Tax Representative holding an active AFM in Greece.

Step-by-Step Instructions​

Step 1. Access the myAADE Application Service​

Open the online application portal: apoktisiafm.azurewebsites.net.

Step 2. Fill In Personal Details​

Enter your details using Latin script exactly as printed on your passport:

  • First Name and Last Name
  • Father's Name and Mother's Name
  • Date of Birth and Country of Birth
  • Passport / ID Number
  • Residential Address in Greece

Example input format:

First Name: GEORGIOS
Last Name: PAPADOPOULOS
Country of Birth: GEORGIA / KAZAKHSTAN / RUSSIA
Document: PASSPORT N1234567

Step 3. Upload Identity Proof​

Attach a clear scan of your passport data page and legal residence document.

Step 4. Identity Verification via myAADElive​

Select an available date and time slot for a short video call with an AADE tax officer. Present your physical passport during the call.

Step 5. Receive AFM & Taxisnet Credentials (Kleidarithmos)​

Upon approval, you will receive:

  • AFM Confirmation Certificate (Βεβαίωση Απόδοσης ΑΦΜ) — Sent as a PDF to your email.
  • Taxisnet Credentials (Kleidarithmos / Κλειδάριθμος) — Delivered in two separate parts:
    • Part 1: Sent via SMS to your mobile phone.
    • Part 2: Sent via E-mail.

Use these credentials to complete initial login on the AADE Taxisnet portal and establish your permanent password.

Frequently Asked Questions​

Is a Tax Representative required?
If you are registered as a non-resident for tax purposes, AADE requires designating a local Tax Representative with an active AFM in Greece.

How long does processing take?
Depending on video appointment availability, issuance takes 1 to 3 business days.

Is there an application fee?
No. Issuance of an AFM and Taxisnet credentials by AADE is completely free.
